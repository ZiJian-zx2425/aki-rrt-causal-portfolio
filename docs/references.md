# Literature and attribution

## The supplied 2022 reference

[Xiao et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2022.820624), *The Efficacy and Safety of Early Renal Replacement Therapy in Critically Ill Patients With Acute Kidney Injury: A Meta-Analysis With Trial Sequential Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials*, addresses the same clinical timing question through evidence synthesis. Its workflow includes trial selection, risk-of-bias assessment, random-effects pooling, heterogeneity and sensitivity analyses, and trial sequential analysis. **TSA is not TTE.** No numerical finding or figure from that paper is presented as a result of this portfolio.

## A closer clinical-methods reference

[She et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2025.1645046), *The early bird in renal rescue: timing matters in acute kidney injury management—insights from target trial emulation*, is cited in the course slides. It is a closer reference for observational RRT-timing analysis. Citation does not establish that the course project implemented every procedure from the paper; this repository does not claim to replicate it.

## Design and implementation references

- [Hernán et al., 2016: Specifying a target trial prevents immortal time bias and other self-inflicted injuries in observational analyses](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27237061/). Design alignment and grace-period concerns.
- [Hernán and Robins: Causal Inference, What If](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/causal-inference-book/). Causal identification, weighting, and longitudinal methods.
- [EconML CausalForestDML documentation](https://www.pywhy.org/EconML/_autosummary/econml.dml.CausalForestDML.html). Residualized causal forests and implementation options.
- [lifelines CoxPHFitter documentation](https://lifelines.readthedocs.io/en/latest/fitters/regression/CoxPHFitter.html). Weighted Cox fitting and robust variance options.

The diagrams are newly drawn. They do not reproduce publisher figures. Clinical reports, course slides, and notebooks are not distributed with this repository.
